ForgeGUI Connect

For Roblox Studio
ForgeGUI Connect is an AI-powered Roblox Studio integration designed to streamline game development by bridging web-based asset generation directly into your workspace. Instead of manually downloading, managing, and importing generated assets or copying code snippets, the plugin links your ForgeGUI account directly to Roblox Studio for seamless workflow execution.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Later
  • Roblox Studio
  • ForgeGUI Pro Tier
